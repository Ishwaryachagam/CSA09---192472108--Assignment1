2. INTEGRATED SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
The integrated system synthesizes all foundational object-oriented principles established in Tasks 1 through 10:

Core Structural Components:
1. `ParkingSlot`:
   - Attributes: `floor`, `slotIndex`, `isChargingEquipped`, `occupiedVehicle`, `activeTicket`.
   - Methods: `isCompatibleWith(Vehicle v)`, `park(Vehicle v, ParkingTicket t)`, `vacate()`.
2. `Vehicle` Hierarchy:
   - `abstract class Vehicle`: Roots identity (`plateNumber`), ownership (`ownerType`), and abstract requirements.
   - `TwoWheeler`: Compact footprint, ₹10/hr tariff.
   - `Car`: Standard parking, ₹20/hr tariff.
   - `ElectricCar`: Inherits from `Car`, implements `Chargeable`. Distinguishes between standard EV parking and active charging bay requests (₹15/hr electricity surcharge).
3. `CampusUser` and Pass Validation:
   - Identifies user category: "STUDENT", "STAFF", "VISITOR".
   - Business Rule: Students and staff presenting a valid monthly pass park at ₹0.00. Visitors pay standard tariffs.
4. `ParkingTicket`:
   - Generated with unique monotonic IDs from an atomic sequence counter.
   - Captures arrival and departure timestamps.
   - Stores itemized fee breakdown (base parking fee, charging surcharge, pass discount).
5. `SmartParkingFacility`:
   - Manages jagged array `ParkingSlot[floors][]`.
   - Allocates the lowest-available compatible slot.
   - Releases slots safely, returning itemized checkout receipts.

5. SYSTEM DEFENCE AND CRITICAL EVALUATION (BLOOM'S LEVEL: K5)
================================================================================

A. Architectural Soundness:
- Loose Coupling & High Cohesion: Each entity is single-purposed. Vehicle pricing rules are encapsulated inside vehicle classes. Facility slots are decoupled from specific vehicle implementations through polymorphism.
- Zero Modification for Future Vehicles: If a new vehicle type (e.g. `ElectricScooter` or `CampusShuttle`) is introduced, it extends `Vehicle` without modifying any existing lines in `SmartParkingFacility`.

B. Robustness Against Real-World Edge Cases:
1. Partial Hour Billing: The ceiling formula `Math.ceil(minutes / 60.0)` prevents university revenue loss from integer truncation.
2. Slot Contamination Prevention: Electric vehicles needing charge are prohibited from occupying standard non-charging bays, and non-EVs are deprioritized from occupying charging bays.
3. Tamper Resistance: All core ticket timestamps and IDs are declared `final`. Slot matrices are shielded behind private encapsulation.
4. Concurrency Preparedness: Monotonic static ticket counter and thread-safe slot assignments can be synchronized or backed by atomic data structures for multi-barrier campus gates.

C. Complexity Profile:
- Allocation: O(F * S) worst-case linear search, O(1) best case.
- Release: O(1) instant direct-indexed validation and release.
- Space Complexity: O(sum(S_i)) physical slot allocations with zero jagged array overhead.
